## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Hay Day** is a captivating farm management game that allows you to run your own farm and make it flourish right from your Windows desktop. Developed by Supercell, the creators of popular games like Clash of Clans, Hay Day brings the charm of rural life and farming directly to your PC. 

With **Hay Day**, you can enjoy all the features of the mobile version while utilizing the comfort of a keyboard and mouse. The **latest version** offers a complete experience, making it easy to engage in farming activities, manage your crops, and interact with friends—all in one place.

<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Create Your Own Farm** — Design and expand your farm, growing crops and raising animals.
- **Manage Your Sales** — Set up a sales point to buy and sell your products efficiently.
